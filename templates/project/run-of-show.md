## Before the talk (5 min)
1. Copy the whole extracted folder (or the zip, then right-click > Extract All). Never present from inside the zip.
2. Double-click `index.html`; open it in **Chrome or Edge**. No internet and no server needed.
3. Press **F** for fullscreen (refused: F11). Press **S** for the presenter window, drag it to the laptop screen (allow pop-ups).
4. Presenter window: **T** starts the timer.
5. Media slide: press **K** once to check sound and volume, then **J** + **K** to restart and pause — or reload.
6. Keep `backup-reading.pdf` open in another window; the video files are in `assets/media` for any player.
