# Credit

The motion *ideas* in this folder — hold after the key moment, acceleration instead of linear speed, one hero, a technique starring once, clipped glints, legibility before camera, deterministic motion, and the named shots (line-carry, deal-in, match-iris, page-turn, mosaic reframe, draw-trace, and others) — are adapted from [video-shotcraft](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft) by Vincent Wei, licensed Apache-2.0.

This deck does not include that project's Remotion/timeline source, demo scenes, or media. Cards here are rewritten for a step-based HTML presentation: one Next plays a short WAAPI move, the speaker holds the rest, and the CSS end state is what static/PDF mode shows. Frame counts from the 30fps films were not copied as durations.
